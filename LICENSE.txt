PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: February 11, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

RecoilApp ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This
Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what
choices you have regarding your data when you use RecoilApp ("the Service").
RecoilApp is a self-hosted platform operated by the RecoilApp team.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

2.1 Information You Provide
- Account Data: Username, email address, and password (stored as a bcrypt
  hash — we never store plain-text passwords).
- Profile Data: Display name, avatar image, banner image, custom status, bio,
  and accent color — all optional.
- Messages: Text content, file attachments, reactions, and thread replies you
  send through the Service.
- Server Data: Servers you create or join, channels, roles, and community
  settings.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- Presence Data: Online/offline/idle/DND status for real-time presence
  features.
- Session Tokens: JWT access tokens (15-minute expiry) and refresh tokens
  (7-day expiry) for authentication.
- Timestamps: Account creation date, message timestamps, and last online time.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the information we collect solely to:
- Operate and maintain the Service (deliver messages, manage servers,
  authenticate users).
- Provide real-time features (typing indicators, presence tracking,
  notifications).
- Enforce community standards and moderate content (audit logs, ban records).
- Send transactional emails (email verification, password resets) — no
  marketing emails.
- Improve security (rate limiting, login monitoring, two-factor
  authentication).

4. DATA SECURITY

We take the security of your data seriously and implement multiple layers of
protection including:
- Encryption in Transit (TLS)
- Password Security (bcrypt hashing)
- Two-Factor Authentication (TOTP-based MFA)
- Security Headers (HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options)
- Rate Limiting
- Input Sanitization (XSS, SQL injection, HPP prevention)

5. DATA SHARING

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties — ever.

RecoilApp is self-hosted and does not use third-party analytics, advertising

required by law.

6. DATA RETENTION

- Account Data: Retained until you delete your account. Upon deletion, your

- Session Tokens: Access tokens expire after 15 minutes. Refresh tokens expire

- File Uploads: Removed when the associated content is deleted.
